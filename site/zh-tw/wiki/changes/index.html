Terror tiers

原本現在
The climb · rule 7—A tier’s end screen replays it at once: REPLAY TERROR and the tier, or R, starts it again as the waypoint would, with new affixes. After a replay it comes first, so replays chain. It shows only for a tier your climb has cleared, never for a Hardcore hero who died.
The climb · rule 8—A tier’s portal waits for you however long you are away, so the boss’s loot is never left unseen. The tier was cleared as he fell.

Stats

原本現在
The character screen · rule 5—The arrows at the stats’ bottom left turn to three more pages, as the PlugY mod adds to Diablo II’s: Offense (+skills, -% to enemy resistances, speeds, critical strike and what your hits do), Defense (Damage Reduced, block, each resistance with its cap and its total before the cap, absorbs, life) and Finds and Breakpoints.
The character screen · rule 6—The breakpoints are the Faster Hit Recovery and Faster Block Rate where your hit recovery or block gets a frame shorter, for your class’s own animation, and the next one up in green. Faster Cast Rate has none here: every point of it shortens your cooldowns.

Charsi’s Forge

原本現在
The Forge at a glance · introCharsi improves your items at her anvil, paid with the Burning Essence of Terror, which only Terror tiers drop. Temper raises a random line of an item, with a lower chance the more tempers it holds. Clean removes every temper. The Hellforge, once per item, doubles them all or loses them all.
